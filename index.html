All FAQs

How accurate is the instant fence estimate?

It's a ballpark range based on the material, height, length, gates and extras you choose. The exact line, slopes, tree roots, rock, access and permits can change the final price, which is confirmed in writing after a site visit.

How deep do you set fence posts?

Deep enough to sit below the frost line, on a gravel base for drainage, with concrete shaped so frost can't lift it. In our heavy clay soil that matters more than any other part of the fence.

Do I need a permit for a fence?

It depends on your municipality and the fence. Some towns require a permit for every fence, others only for pool enclosures, and all of them limit heights. We confirm the rules for your address before building.

Who finds out where buried utilities are?

We request utility locates before every job. The service is free, and no digging starts until the gas, hydro, cable and water lines near the fence line are marked.
