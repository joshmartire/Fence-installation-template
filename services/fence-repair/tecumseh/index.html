Fence Repair for Tecumseh homes

Tecumseh stretches from lakeside neighbourhoods on Lake St. Clair to the Oldcastle industrial area and farmland in the former Sandwich South township. Wind off Lake St. Clair is the leading cause of fence damage in north Tecumseh, while rural properties see more damage from frost heave and equipment. We rebuild fallen sections and reset heaved posts with drainage gravel and concrete shaped to resist frost.

Local conditions in Tecumseh

Lakeside fences in Tecumseh deal with wind damage and damp air, while rural fences see frost heave, equipment damage and overgrown fence rows. Repairs, gate rebuilds and staining are planned around each property's exposure.

Questions to ask before you hire

Whoever you hire for fence repair in Tecumseh, get clear answers to these before signing:

  • Is the problem the post, the panel or the gate?
  • Do the posts on either side need work too?
  • Is repair or replacement the better value?
  • How closely can you match my existing fence?

By-laws, permits and locates

Tecumseh asks homeowners to get a fence permit, make sure the fence is on their own property and book a final inspection. Fences can be up to 1.9 metres in rear and side yards and 1.22 metres in front yards. Pool fences must be at least four feet high, not climbable and fitted with spring-loaded, self-latching gates on the pool side. Rules change, so we confirm current requirements with the Town and request utility locates before digging.
